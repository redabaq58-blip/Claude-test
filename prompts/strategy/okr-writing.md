For each goal, create a proper OKR following these rules:

OBJECTIVE rules:
- Qualitative and inspirational — not a metric
- Answers "where are we going?"
- Ambitious but not impossible
- Time-bound (quarterly)
- Written so a new employee would understand why it matters

KEY RESULT rules:
- Measurable with a specific number and baseline
- Outcome-focused — not tasks or outputs
- 2–5 KRs per Objective
- 70% achievement = success (ambitious but realistic)
- Each KR has a clear starting point and target

Format:
OBJECTIVE: [inspirational statement]
KR1: [metric] from [baseline] to [target] by [date]
KR2: [metric] from [baseline] to [target] by [date]
KR3: [metric] from [baseline] to [target] by [date]
ALIGNMENT: [how this connects to company-level priorities]

**MEASUREMENT PLAN**
