Create a detailed 30/60/90 day onboarding plan for the following new hire.

**New hire name:** {{new_hire_name}}

**Role:** {{role_title}}

**Team:** {{team_name}}

**Manager:** {{manager_name}}

**Start date:** {{start_date}}

**Key objectives for the role:** {{role_objectives}}

**Current team context:** {{team_context}}

Structure the plan as follows for each phase:

**FIRST 30 DAYS — LEARN**
Goal: Understand the business, team, tools, and context before contributing.
- Week 1: [specific onboarding activities]
- Week 2: [meetings, deep dives, shadowing]
- Week 3–4: [first contributions, relationship building]
- Success metrics: what does "done" look like at day 30?
- Key people to meet: [list with context for each]

**DAYS 31–60 — CONTRIBUTE**
Goal: Start delivering real value with increasing autonomy.
- Key projects or tasks to own
- Skills to develop
- Processes to learn
- Success metrics for day 60

**DAYS 61–90 — LEAD**
Goal: Operating independently and beginning to drive impact.
- Ownership areas fully handed over
- Goals set for rest of quarter
- Success metrics for day 90

**CHECK-IN CADENCE**
Suggested 1:1 topics for manager check-ins at week 1, 2, 4, 8, and 12.

**ONBOARDING RESOURCES**
Key documents, tools, and training to prioritize (with suggested order).
