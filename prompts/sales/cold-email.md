Write a personalized cold outreach email to the following prospect.

**Prospect details:**
- Name: {{prospect_name}}
- Title: {{prospect_title}}
- Company: {{prospect_company}}
- Industry: {{industry}}
- Company size: {{company_size}}

**Our product/service:** {{product_description}}

**Their likely pain point:** {{pain_point}}

Requirements:
- Use AIDA structure (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
- Personalized opening hook relevant to their role and company
- One specific value proposition tied to their likely pain
- Single low-friction CTA (e.g., "Worth a 15-minute call?")
- Subject line: curiosity-driven, under 50 characters, no spam words
- Body: 80–120 words maximum
- Tone: peer-to-peer, human, not "salesy"
- Never use: "I hope this email finds you well", "synergy", "circle back", "reach out"

Output format:
Subject: [subject line]

[email body]
