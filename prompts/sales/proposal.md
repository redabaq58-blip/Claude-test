- Prospect company: {{prospect_company}}

- Pricing: {{pricing}}
- Deal context: {{deal_context}}

**Their key business problems:** {{key_problems}}

**Our differentiators:** {{differentiators}}

Structure the proposal as follows:

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
One page. The business case in plain language. What we're proposing, why it matters, and what it costs.

**UNDERSTANDING YOUR SITUATION**
Demonstrate we listened. Summarize their challenges in their own terms.

**PROPOSED SOLUTION**
What we're delivering. Specific to their needs — not a product brochure.

**ROI & BUSINESS IMPACT**
Quantify the value. Use their numbers where possible. Show payback period.

**INVESTMENT SUMMARY**
Clear pricing table. What's included. What's not. Payment terms.

**IMPLEMENTATION PLAN**
Phased timeline. Key milestones. What they need to do vs. what we handle.

**WHY US**
2–3 specific differentiators relevant to their situation. One relevant case study or reference.

**NEXT STEPS**
Specific: names, dates, actions. Remove ambiguity from the path forward.

Tone: confident, clear, client-focused. Every section answers "what's in it for them?"
