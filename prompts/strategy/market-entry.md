Deliver a structured market entry analysis:

**MARKET ATTRACTIVENESS**
- Market size (TAM/SAM) estimate with basis
- Growth rate and trajectory
- Competitive intensity (fragmented vs. concentrated)
- Regulatory/compliance barriers
- Cultural or operational complexity
- Overall attractiveness rating: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

**COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE**
- Key players and their positioning
- Our differentiation vs. incumbents
- Potential competitive responses to our entry

**ENTRY STRATEGY OPTIONS**
Evaluate 2–3 entry modes:
Option A: [e.g., direct sales, partnership, acquisition, product-led]
- Pros / Cons / Time to revenue / Resource requirements

**GO-TO-MARKET APPROACH**
- Ideal customer profile in this market
- Primary acquisition channels
- Pricing strategy (same, adjusted, or new model?)
- Localization requirements

**FINANCIAL PROJECTIONS (HIGH LEVEL)**
- Investment required (Year 1)
- Break-even timeline
- Revenue potential (Year 3 realistic case)

**RISKS & MITIGATION**
Top 5 risks and how to mitigate them.

**RECOMMENDATION**
Go / No-go / Go with conditions — and the 3 most critical success factors.
