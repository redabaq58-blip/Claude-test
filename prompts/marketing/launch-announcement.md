**What we're launching:** {{product_or_feature}}

**Key benefits (top 3):** {{key_benefits}}

**Any special offer or urgency:** {{special_offer}}

Deliverables:

**BLOG POST ANNOUNCEMENT**
- Headline: compelling, benefit-led
- Introduction: what it is and why it matters (hook in first sentence)
- The problem it solves: customer pain point framing
- How it works: clear, jargon-free explanation
- Key benefits: 3–5 specific, outcome-focused bullets
- Social proof or quote (placeholder if none available)
- CTA: clear next step with link placeholder
- Length: 400–600 words

**EMAIL ANNOUNCEMENT** (to existing customers/subscribers)
- Subject line: [2 variations — benefit-led and curiosity-led]
- Preview text: [<90 chars]
- Body: personal, direct, excited but not hypey. 150–200 words.
- CTA: one clear action

**SOCIAL MEDIA POSTS**
- LinkedIn: professional tone, 150 words, includes hook + value + CTA
- Twitter/X: 240 chars, punchy, includes relevant hashtags
- Short-form (Instagram/Facebook): 80 words, visual-first framing
