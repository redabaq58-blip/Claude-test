# Initial Research Prompt Template

Use this template to kick off a structured research task:

```
Research the following topic thoroughly: {{topic}}

Please provide:
1. A concise executive summary (2-3 sentences)
2. 5-7 key findings with source context
3. Analysis of trends and implications
4. Known gaps or limitations in available information
5. Recommended next steps for deeper investigation

Confidence level: indicate HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW for each major claim.
```
