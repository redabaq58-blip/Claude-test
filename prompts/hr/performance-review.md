Write a professional performance review based on the following notes.

**Employee:** {{employee_name}}

**Role:** {{employee_role}}

**Review period:** {{review_period}}

**Rating:** {{overall_rating}} (e.g., Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement)

**Key achievements (bullet notes):**
{{achievements_notes}}

**Areas for improvement (bullet notes):**
{{improvement_notes}}

**Behavioral examples:**
{{behavioral_examples}}

**Development goals for next period:**
{{development_goals}}

Write a complete performance review with the following structure:

**OVERALL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY**
2–3 sentence narrative summary that sets the tone for the review.

**KEY ACHIEVEMENTS**
4–6 specific, quantified achievements. Each follows: what they did → the impact → why it mattered.

**AREAS FOR GROWTH**
2–3 specific areas. Each follows: the gap → why it matters → what improvement looks like.

**BEHAVIORAL HIGHLIGHTS**
2–3 SBI (Situation-Behavior-Impact) examples from the notes provided.

**DEVELOPMENT GOALS — NEXT PERIOD**
2–3 concrete, measurable goals with suggested success criteria.

**RATING RATIONALE**
2–3 sentences explaining why this rating is appropriate, tied to evidence.

Requirements:
- Third-person, professional tone
- Specific over vague — no "great team player" without examples
- Balanced — even strong performers have growth areas
- Avoid recency bias — address the full review period
