Improve for:
1. **Clarity** — eliminate jargon, ambiguity, and overly complex sentences
2. **Conciseness** — cut unnecessary words without losing meaning
3. **Flow** — ensure smooth transitions and logical progression
4. **Impact** — stronger opening, memorable closing
5. **Tone** — consistent voice matching the target audience

Provide:
1. **Improved version** — the edited text
2. **Key changes** — 3-5 bullet points explaining the most important improvements
3. **What was preserved** — important elements kept intentionally

Do not change the author's core message or voice — enhance, don't replace.
