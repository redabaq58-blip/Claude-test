Perform a competitive analysis for:

**My Product/Company:** {{my_product}}
**Competitors to analyze:** {{competitors}}
**Market/Space:** {{market}}

For each competitor, analyze:
1. **Core offering** — what they do, their key features
2. **Target customer** — who they serve and how
3. **Pricing model** — how they make money
4. **Strengths** — what they do exceptionally well
5. **Weaknesses** — where they fall short or have gaps

Then synthesize:

## Competitive Landscape Summary
Overall picture of the competitive space.

## Key Differentiators
How do these players differentiate from each other?

## Market Gaps
What customer needs are underserved by current players?

## Strategic Opportunities
Where can {{my_product}} win? What positioning makes sense?

## Threats to Watch
What could hurt my position?

Be brutally honest — sugarcoating helps no one.
