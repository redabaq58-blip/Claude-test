Write a 3-touch follow-up email sequence for a prospect who hasn't responded to our initial outreach.

**Context:**
- Prospect: {{prospect_name}}, {{prospect_title}} at {{prospect_company}}
- Initial email topic: {{initial_email_topic}}
- Product/service: {{product_description}}
- Time since initial email: {{days_since_initial}} days

Requirements:
- Each email adds NEW value — never just "checking in"
- Progressively shorter as the sequence progresses
- Follow-up 1: Share a relevant insight, case study, or resource
- Follow-up 2: Different angle — ask a question or challenge an assumption
- Follow-up 3: Graceful breakup email that leaves the door open
- Each email: unique subject line, 60–100 words
- Tone stays warm throughout — no guilt-tripping

Format:
FOLLOW-UP 1 (3–5 days after initial)
Subject: [subject]
[body]

FOLLOW-UP 2 (5–7 days after follow-up 1)
Subject: [subject]
[body]

FOLLOW-UP 3 — Breakup (7–10 days after follow-up 2)
Subject: [subject]
[body]
